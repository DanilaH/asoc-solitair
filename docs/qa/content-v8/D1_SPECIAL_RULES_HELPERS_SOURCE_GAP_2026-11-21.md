## 2. Two non-interchangeable original algorithm phases

**Hitapps Shuffle phase (order 20/25/27, not unlock levels):**
- `Lockers`: `config.enable`, native minLevel, `LockerPattern>=0`, `FeatureLevelSeed>0`, **bounded list-index** lookup; `LevelDto.LevelType` result bits applied=2, failed=4.
- `Frozen`: `config.enable`, `FrozenPattern>=0`, `FeatureLevelSeed>0`, **lookup by pattern.id**, applied=32, failed=64.
- `Bound`: `config.enable`, `BoundPattern>=0`, `FeatureLevelSeed>0`, **lookup by pattern.id**, applied=128, failed=256.
- Shared `LevelFeatureContext.LevelShuffled` causes **at most one** eligible `FeatureLevelSeed` reseed in the fresh path. A pattern may fail **after** that reseed, and the candidate scorer has no explicit failed-flag veto in the inspected path. Thus a failed modifier must not be reported as “original initial board unchanged” or necessarily rejected.

Source local map evidence: **219/226** bundled inputs have no native locker/frozen/bound override; only **7** English local positions (18/21/23/25/26/29/31) have locker+feature seed; **0** bundled Frozen/Bound overrides. This is **not** a proof of their production absence (remote replacement and active config unknown), and those positions are not necessarily public user level numbers.

**Hitapps Final phase (order 30/45, after simulation):**
- Gold is gated through `TryGetGoldRushGoldenCardAmount(level,out amount)` and first-gold state; `GoldenCardsFeatureApplier` may update gold counters/level type and callback. **Gold-marked special cards are not the same as a Gold Rush event payout or standard win coins.**
- `StarterBoosterFeatureApplier` resolves selected booster IDs, then draws new **UnityEngine.Random.Range(0,Int32.MaxValue)** seed and inserts Magnet/Hammer in stock via its own native operations. It does **not** use the ordinary candidate's `EmbeddedRandom` continuation.
- Generic `tools/content/candidate-pipeline.ts` has named phase hooks, but **that is an offline source-shaped adapter only**; the actual C8 `Board`, Rules3 and published deals have no Frozen/Bound/Golden/Hitapps StarterBooster fields.

## 4. QA and migration negative controls

- Every modifier must have a **separate feature key and emitted immutable Deal identity**: one frozen+keys combination is not source-proof of all combinations. New rule state and all Undo snapshots must serialize; resume old C1–C8 board under its historic Rules version. New effects must never be paid for twice after activity pause, app kill or duplicate earned callback.
- Forced Shuffle cannot silently turn a previously certified winning route into a `UNKNOWN` without explicitly replacing the Deal ID and independent full winning replay. For levels with large stock and five tableau columns, never run a full solver on the touch/animation UI thread.
- For original Frozen and Bound, first recover the actual meaning of a layer / bound / scissors action (can require multiple taps, moves, or conditions): don't guess “tap ice three times” from metadata. For original AZUR Joker, don't erase the whole Undo history merely because an original source path does; preserve current product invariants and require versioned explicit decision.
- Distinguish initial/Shuffle/Final RNG and `LevelType` flags. Post-seed injection of starter boosters or gold makes pre-Final candidate hashes insufficient for actual final board parity.
- Record original/source SHA, product commit, deal ID, active budget, actions/rejected moves, screenshot/phone/SDK proof and human result separately.
